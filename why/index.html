Why 11ov3

We refuse the normalization of famine as a weapon of war. 11ov3 exists to turn story into action: to unite citizens, faith leaders, and artists to feed children and to press governments to act under international law.

  • Because children cannot wait while leaders stall.
  • Because memory is a duty: our peoples knew famine; we will not allow it for others.
  • Because law exists for the moments it is hardest to uphold.
