Email Your Ambassador

Copy, personalize, and send:

Subject: Request an Emergency Special Session under Resolution 377

Dear Ambassador [Name],
Gaza aid vetoes have created Security Council deadlock. Under UNGA Resolution 377 (Uniting for Peace), a majority of member states can request an Emergency Special Session within 24 hours. We urge [COUNTRY] to sign the request and support an agenda to:
1) Open humanitarian corridors “by all available means”;
2) Mobilize funding for WFP, UNICEF, OCHA;
3) Call on member states to facilitate access and protection.
From Gaza to Sudan, Congo, and Somaliland, famine is being used as a weapon of war. Please act so no child’s hunger is used as a bargaining chip.
Respectfully,
[Your name, city]

Call Script (MP / FM)

Short and clear:

“I’m calling to ask [Official] to authorize our UN Ambassador to request an Emergency Special Session under Resolution 377. Gaza vetoes created deadlock. The GA can act if 97 countries request it. Please support corridors for aid and funding to end famine-by-war.”
