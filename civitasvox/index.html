Why Myth?

Stories are how we see the world, how we share what matters. Myth makes imagination real. In Civitasvox, we carry forward a living myth: not to escape the world, but to repair it. Every verse, every shard, every stream is part of the same call: feed the children, end famine as a weapon of war.
